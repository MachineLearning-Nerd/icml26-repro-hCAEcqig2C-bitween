\[
f(x)=\frac{L}{1+e^{-k\,(x-x_0)}}
\]

is the classic logistic (sigmoid) function.  
Define the **logit** transform  

\[
\operatorname{logit}(x)=\ln\!\frac{f(x)}{L-f(x)} .
\]

Because  

\[
\frac{f(x)}{L-f(x)}=\frac{L}{L-f(x)}-1
       =\frac{1}{e^{-k\,(x-x_0)}}=e^{k\,(x-x_0)},
\]

we have  

\[
\operatorname{logit}(x)=k\,(x-x_0).
\]

Thus the logit is an **affine** function of the input.  
From this simple linearity we can obtain many identities that relate
values of \(f\) at correlated points.

---

### 1.  Additive composition  

For any random shift \(r\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{logit}(x+r)
   &=k\bigl((x+r)-x_0\bigr)                                   \\
   &=k\,(x-x_0)+k\,(r-x_0)                                   \\
   &=\operatorname{logit}(x)+\operatorname{logit}(r)+k\,x_0 .
\end{aligned}
\]

Exponentiating returns to the original function:

\[
\frac{f(x+r)}{L-f(x+r)}
   =\frac{f(x)}{L-f(x)}\,
    \frac{f(r)}{L-f(r)}\,
    e^{k\,x_0}.
\]

After clearing denominators we obtain the first verified property

\[
f(x+r)\,(L-f(x))\,(L-f(r))
   \;-\;(L-f(x+r))\,f(x)\,f(r)\,e^{k\,x_0}=0 .
\tag{A}
\]

Writing the same identity with the “logit” form gives a compact
alternative expression:

\[
\frac{L}{f(x+r)}-1
   \;-\;\Bigl(\frac{L}{f(x)}-1\Bigr)
        \Bigl(\frac{L}{f(r)}-1\Bigr)\,e^{k\,x_0}=0 .
\tag{B}
\]

Both (A) and (B) are exact consequences of the linearity of the logit.

---

### 2.  Symmetry around the centre \(x_0\)  

Because \(e^{k\,(x_0-d)}=e^{-k\,d}\),

\[
f(x_0+d)=\frac{L}{1+e^{-k d}},\qquad
f(x_0-d)=\frac{L}{1+e^{k d}} .
\]

Adding the two expressions yields the constant \(L\):

\[
f(x_0+d)+f(x_0-d)=L .
\]

Replacing \(d\) by \(x-x_0\) gives a simple even‑symmetry property

\[
f(x)+f\bigl(2x_0-x\bigr)-L=0 .
\tag{C}
\]

---

### 3.  Subtractive composition
